PICO: THE MARTIAN SURVIVOR

The year is 2030. An expedition led by Captain Vijay with crew members Sristi, Bhavyesh, Bikramaditya, Anurag, and Saswati reaches Mars — but an asteroid storm tears the spacecraft apart. Young astronaut Pico survives a crash landing, alone on the Red Planet. Guided by Captain Vijay through a failing signal, Pico must learn to survive, explore real Martian regions, brave underground dungeons, and repair his spacecraft to find a way home.

Explore real Mars biomes and historic landing sites including Gusev Crater (Spirit, MER-A, 2004 · USA), Meridiani Planum (Opportunity, MER-B, 2004 · USA), Gale Crater, Jezero Crater, and Valles Marineris.

SURVIVE

Manage oxygen, power, water, food, radiation, and suit integrity. Use habitats, stations, shelters, and supplies to stay alive.

EXPLORE

Cross real Martian regions and visit rover landing sites, discover structures, and investigate underground dungeons for vital resources.

RETURN

Overcome setbacks, repair your spacecraft with Captain Vijay's guidance, launch from Mars, and reunite with the crew.
