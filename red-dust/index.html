The Story

A stranded astronaut, a fragile signal, and a way home

A Mars expedition led by Captain Vijay ends in disaster when an asteroid storm strikes. Pico survives the crash — but must face Mars alone, guided only by a weak communication link.

Main character: Pico

Captain: Captain Vijay

Crew: Sristi, Bhavyesh, Bikramaditya, Anurag, Saswati

Mission: Survive the Red Planet, overcome unexpected problems, explore unknown terrain and underground dungeons, repair the damaged spacecraft, and launch back to the crew.

Manage vital survival systems: oxygen, power, water, food, radiation, and suit integrity. Every decision matters.

Travel across real Martian regions, discover historic rover/lander mission sites, and press E to open educational mission info popups (resources pause while you learn).

From the crash site to the final launch, Pico’s journey is a story of courage, learning, and determination.

Real Mars Locations & Missions

Explore real regions and historic mission sites

The game includes real Martian biomes/regions and interactive mission markers with educational popups (press E in-game).

Real Martian Regions

Gusev Crater, Meridiani Planum, Jezero Crater, Gale Crater, and Valles Marineris — represented as distinct in-game biomes/terrain zones.

Educational Popups

Approach mission markers and press E to open an eye-catching info popup. Resources pause so you can read and learn comfortably.

🇺🇸

Spirit (MER-A) · Gusev Crater ↗

2004 · NASA (Mars Exploration Rover)

Landed Jan 4, 2004 to explore geology and signs of past water; operated until 2010, far beyond its planned mission.
🇺🇸

Opportunity (MER-B) · Meridiani Planum ↗

2004 · NASA (Mars Exploration Rover)

Landed Jan 25, 2004 (“Oppy”); studied geology and past water evidence; operated 5,111 sols until 2018.
🇺🇸

Phoenix Lander · Near Martian North Pole ↗

2008 · NASA / Univ. of Arizona (LPL) · JPL

Landed May 25, 2008 to dig for water ice and study habitability/history of water; operated 157 sols (161 days).
🇺🇸

Curiosity (MSL) · Gale Crater ↗

2012 · NASA/JPL

Car-sized rover exploring Gale Crater and Mount Sharp since 2012, studying climate, geology, and past habitable environments.
🇺🇸

Perseverance (Mars 2020) · Jezero Crater ↗

2021 · NASA/JPL

Exploring Jezero Crater since 2021 to search for signs of ancient life, collect samples, study geology/climate, and test technologies for future human exploration (including Ingenuity’s first powered flight on another planet).
